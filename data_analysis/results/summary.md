## 3. Health topics

A post counts toward a topic if its title or body contains at least one of the topic's keywords as a whole word. The keywords are listed in section 5. A post can count toward several topics. The counts show that a topic is mentioned. They do not show that it is the main subject of the post.

| Topic | Posts | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Mental and emotional health | 292 | 80.9% |
| Sexual and reproductive health | 41 | 11.4% |
| Physical symptoms and medical care | 38 | 10.5% |
| Fitness and nutrition | 25 | 6.9% |
| Skin and appearance | 24 | 6.6% |
| Body image and eating | 22 | 6.1% |
| Substances | 15 | 4.2% |

Mental and emotional health is defined broadly. It includes posts that mention loneliness, sadness, stress, crying, venting, coping, addiction or using a chatbot as a therapist.

Posts matching two or more topics: 91 (25.2%). Posts matching no topic: 27 (7.5%).

Posts that mention suicide or self-harm: 56 (15.5%). The count includes mentions about another person and statements such as "I am not suicidal". These posts are also counted under mental and emotional health.
