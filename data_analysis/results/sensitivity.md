# Sensitivity of the topic counts to the keyword lists

All counts are out of 361 posts. The tables show how the counts in summary.md change under specific stricter matching rules. They measure dependence on the keyword lists. They are not bounds, and they do not measure whether a matched post is truly about the topic or how many posts on a topic use none of its keywords.

## 1. Stricter rules

All keywords: the count in summary.md, where one keyword is enough. Two or more keywords: the post must contain at least two different keywords of the topic. Without the top keyword: the count after removing the keyword on which the most posts rest alone. Different forms of a word, such as cry and crying, are separate keywords.
